# W.U.T.?.6.12 — Blood Type Diet Quick-Reference Ebook

A mobile-friendly, offline-ready quick-reference guide based on Dr. Peter J. D'Adamo's *Eat Right 4 Your Type*.

## What's Inside

- **Type O** — The Hunter (High Protein)
- **Type A** — The Cultivator (Vegetarian)
- **Type B** — The Nomad (Balanced)
- **Type AB** — The Enigma (Mixed)

Each section includes:
- Meats & Poultry
- Seafood
- Dairy & Eggs
- Oils & Fats
- Nuts & Seeds
- Beans & Legumes
- Grains & Pasta
- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Beverages & Condiments
- Herbs & Spices
- Supplements

Foods are color-coded:
- 🟢 **Optimal** — Highly beneficial
- 🟡 **Moderate** — Neutral
- 🔴 **Noxious** — Avoid

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in any web browser on your phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Tap your blood type to jump to that section.
3. Tap any category button (Meats, Seafood, Veggies, etc.) to jump directly to that food list.
4. Tap the small "↑ O Cover" button at the bottom of any category to return to your blood type cover.
5. Toggle between blood types using the colored buttons on each cover page.

## Works Offline

This ebook is a single HTML file with no external dependencies. Save it to your phone and it works in airplane mode — perfect for checking at restaurants!
